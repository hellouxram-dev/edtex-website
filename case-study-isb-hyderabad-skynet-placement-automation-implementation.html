Overview

In 2023, ISB Hyderabad and Mohali replaced its legacy placements platform with a customised deployment of Skynet, built to match the institution’s own placement workflows. Students, the placement office and recruiters at both campuses now work on one automated system in a dedicated cloud environment.

Skynet was customised for ISB’s requirements, automating everything from student profiles and CV generation to dream offer workflows, interview scheduling and more than 50 custom analytics dashboards.

More on the product: Skynet Placement Automation.

A legacy system holding the process back

ISB’s placement process is not a standard workflow. It has detailed requirements, from dream offer management and pre-placement talk tracking to access controls for different roles, that generic enterprise software cannot accommodate.

The legacy platform had become a bottleneck. It could not scale with the institution, lacked the depth of automation required and needed manual intervention at every stage. ISB needed a platform built around its process, not the other way round.

Where the old platform fell short

  • A legacy platform unable to support the depth of ISB’s placement workflows
  • CV generation, shortlisting and offer management done by hand
  • No central system for dream offer workflows and recruiter management
  • Limited analytics and reporting for the placement office
  • Hyderabad and Mohali not coordinated on one platform
  • Heavy administrative work for invoicing, queries and access control

A Skynet instance built for ISB

EDTEX deployed a customised instance of Skynet at ISB, designed with the placement office. It automates more than 16 placement workflows across the full recruitment cycle.

Student profiles

Master profile creation, data validation and an e-profile book.

CVs in ISB’s format

CVs generated automatically in the institution’s format.

Pre-placement talks

Event management with notifications and attendance tracking.

Applications and interviews

Applications and shortlists managed, and interviews scheduled across student and recruiter calendars.

Dream offers

Dream offer workflows and offer management.

Recruiter portal and invoicing

Recruiter registration and invoicing workflows in one portal.

Reports and dashboards

More than 50 custom reports and analytics dashboards.

Access, queries and cloud

Custom access by role, a query system for placement season, and a dedicated single-tenant cloud.

Through implementation and operation, the EDTEX team built the platform around ISB’s specific needs and responded promptly to issues, which was central to a successful deployment.
