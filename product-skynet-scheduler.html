Day 0 and Day 1 interview schedules, built by rules and kept accurate all day

The EDTEX Skynet Scheduler slots interviews and group discussions on Day 0, Day 1 and beyond across companies, panels and students, by transparent rules that balance student preferences, interview load and company priorities. At IIT Bombay it scheduled 900 students and 40 companies on Day 0 with zero schedule clashes.

What it does

Day 0 decides who interviews where and when, for many companies at once. The Scheduler replaces overnight manual grids with one engine configured to your placement policy.

Scheduling against four variables

Every student gets a fair, clash-free day.

  • Student preferences for companies and job profiles
  • Each student’s interview load across Days 0 and 1
  • Company preferences for shortlisted students
  • Company start times and panel availability

Cluster cohorts

Parallel processes coordinated rather than competing for the same students.

  • Companies and students organised into clusters
  • Overlapping shortlists coordinated without clashes
  • Rounds and slots sequenced so students move efficiently

Changes and cancellations on the day

Interview day rarely goes to plan; the schedule absorbs it.

  • Company changes and cancellations handled at the job level
  • Affected students notified automatically
  • Rescheduling without disrupting other companies or students

Live tracking and dashboards

Student and interview states tracked in real time on Days 0 and 1: who is interviewing, who is free, who has converted, and where the bottlenecks are.

Fair by design

Slots are allocated by predefined, transparent rules. Every student is processed by the same rules, removing the inconsistency of manual slotting.

Part of Skynet placements

The Scheduler works with Skynet placement operations, so shortlists, interviews and offers share the same data.

How interview day runs

The placement committee sets the day up in advance, then manages it live.

  1. Set up the dayConfigure companies, job profiles, shortlists, panels, slots and start times for Day 0, Day 1 and beyond.
  2. Auto-scheduleGenerate interview and GD schedules that balance student preferences, interview load and company priorities.
  3. Manage liveHandle last-minute changes at the company-job level, with affected students notified automatically.
  4. Track statesMonitor students and interviews in real time: who is interviewing, who is free, who has converted.

Questions about the Skynet Scheduler

Short answers from our team. Anything else, ask us on the discovery call.

See all questions

Can Skynet automate interview scheduling on Day 0 and Day 1?

Yes. Skynet automates scheduling of interviews and group discussions, and tracks student and interview status in real time on high-intensity days such as Day 0 and Day 1. Placement committees see who is where and which rounds are running without juggling spreadsheets.

How does Skynet ensure fair interview slotting?

Skynet allocates interview slots using predefined, transparent rules that weigh student preferences, interview load, recruiter preferences and timing. Every student is processed by the same rules, giving each candidate an equitable chance at their shortlisted companies.

How does Skynet handle last-minute changes by recruiters?

The placement committee can change or cancel a recruiter’s process at the job level at short notice, and Skynet automatically notifies every affected student, so no one is left waiting for updates.

How can students track their applications and interviews?

Students track every application in Skynet in real time, including application status, interview schedules and job offers.

Which placement processes does Skynet support?

Final placements, summer internships, lateral placements, rolling placements, short-term virtual industry projects and industry co-op programmes, each configured with its own eligibility rules, calendar and policies.
