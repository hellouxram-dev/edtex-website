Questions institutions ask before a demo

Is the demo specific to our institution?

Yes. The first call maps how your process runs today. The demonstration that follows is prepared around your academic or placement policy, your rules and, where possible, your data.

How long does implementation take?

Elective course bidding typically goes live in one to four weeks. Timetable automation typically takes four to fifteen weeks, depending on your policies, constraints and the scope of customisation.

Does EDTEX work with our existing systems?

Yes. EDTEX integrates with ERPs and student information systems such as Oracle PeopleSoft, SAP, Ellucian Banner and Anthology Student, and supports single sign-on through Google Workspace and Microsoft Entra ID.

How is our institution's data protected?

Each client has a dedicated cloud tenant, with two-factor authentication, single sign-on, encryption and access controls. EDTEX is designed to support compliance with India's DPDP Act 2023 and the EU GDPR, and every engagement is covered by a data non-disclosure agreement.

What support do we get on critical days?

Our team provides live support during bidding windows, timetable releases and Day 0 placements, alongside training for administrators and guides for students.
