Where academic offices lose weeks every term

The same six problems come up at business schools, law schools and universities as programmes and intake grow. EDTEX was built to remove them, one office at a time.

  1. Spreadsheets and email chains that break at scale

    Critical academic and placement work still runs on spreadsheets, email and shared drives. Add programmes, campuses and intake, and the combinations outgrow any spreadsheet.

  2. Allocating scarce seats fairly, under scrutiny

    Elective seats, exchange places and interview slots are scarce, and every allocation is questioned. The process has to be fair, transparent and fast.

  3. Running many programmes at once

    Shared electives, faculty and rooms across programmes create clashes that only show up after students register.

  4. Scaling placement operations

    Day 0 and Day 1 put hundreds of students, recruiters and interview panels in motion at once, with no room for error.

  5. Disconnected systems

    Data is re-typed between offices, versions conflict, and reports record activity without explaining it.

  6. Being ready for accreditation and rankings

    Leadership needs clean, auditable records of how allocation and placement decisions were made.

What is EDTEX?

EDTEX is a Hyderabad-based software company, founded in 2016, that builds academic operations automation for business schools, law schools and universities. Its products are EDTEX Registro (course bidding, timetabling, student exchange and academic ERP) and EDTEX Skynet (placement automation). EDTEX works with 40+ institutions, including 10% of the global Top 100 business schools.

Which institutions use EDTEX?

EDTEX software is used by 40+ institutions, including IIMs, ISB, XLRI, SDA Bocconi, TAPMI, K J Somaiya and NMIMS, across business schools, universities and law schools in India and internationally.

How long does it take to deploy EDTEX?

Elective course bidding typically goes live in one to four weeks. Timetable automation typically takes four to fifteen weeks, depending on your policies, constraints and the scope of customisation.

Which systems does EDTEX integrate with?

EDTEX integrates with ERPs such as Oracle and SAP, student information systems such as Ellucian and Anthology Student, and Salesforce Education Cloud. Single sign-on works through Google Workspace and Microsoft Entra ID.

How does EDTEX keep institutional and student data secure?

EDTEX uses two-factor authentication, single sign-on, encryption and access controls across Registro and Skynet, with a dedicated cloud tenant for each client. Its platforms are designed to support compliance with India’s DPDP Act 2023 and the EU GDPR.
