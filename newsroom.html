Newsroom

EDTEX in the news

Press coverage of placement seasons that ran on Skynet, including the first fully virtual placements at several of India’s leading institutions in 2020 and 2021.

EDTEX Skynet ran the placement processes reported in nine press articles published between November 2020 and February 2022, in outlets including the Economic Times, Business Standard and The Times of India. They include ISB’s 2,066 offers for the Class of 2022 and XLRI’s 100% placement of all 358 candidates in two days, both during the pandemic.

Where has EDTEX been in the news?

These articles were written and published by the outlets named, and report on each institution's placement results. The placement process at each institution ran on EDTEX Skynet.

  • 3 February 2022
    Economic Times

    ISB’s Class of 2022 receives 2,066 offers

    The entire placement process was conducted in virtual mode because of the pandemic.

    Read the article in Economic Times (opens in a new tab)
  • 25 February 2021
    Business Standard

    XLRI achieves 100% placement within two days

    All 358 candidates of the outgoing 2019-21 batch secured offers in the final recruitment process in a record two days, despite the COVID-19 pandemic.

    Read the article in Business Standard (opens in a new tab)
  • 23 February 2021
    The Times of India

    IIM Kozhikode registers 100% placements in a virtual drive

    The placement process, held virtually, was attended by 137 recruiters, a 4.6% increase on the previous year.

    Read the article in The Times of India (opens in a new tab)
  • 15 December 2020
    The New Indian Express

    IIM Bangalore achieves 100% summer placements

    The entire summer placement process was conducted online, and all 525 students were placed in less than a week.

    Read the article in The New Indian Express (opens in a new tab)
  • 8 December 2020
    Business Standard

    Bain, BCG and Amazon top recruiters as IIM Ahmedabad wraps up summer placements

    Held virtually, the first round covered four cohorts: investment banking and markets, management consulting, advisory consulting, and private equity, venture capital and asset management.

    Read the article in Business Standard (opens in a new tab)
  • 4 December 2020
    The Times of India

    IIT Guwahati: first virtual placements for final-year students

    After two days of the virtual placement drive, the highest domestic package offered was Rs 70 lakh a year, compared with Rs 52 lakh the previous year.

  • 2 December 2020
    The Indian Express

    IIT Madras gets its highest-ever Day 1 offers in a virtual placement drive

    IIT Madras campus placements for 2020-21 saw a rise in the number of offers compared with the previous year.

    The Hindu BusinessLine

    IIT Bombay begins virtual interviews for placements

    Placement interviews for 2,000 final-year students were held entirely online because of COVID-19.

    The process was conducted entirely virtually. Recruiters included Alvarez & Marsal, Amazon, Bain & Co, Citi, Goldman Sachs, JP Morgan Chase, McKinsey & Co and Microsoft.
