Overview

IIM Bangalore deployed two EDTEX products at the same time: Registro Course Bidding for multi-programme elective selection, and Registro Timetabling for data-driven timetable management. Together they cover the cycle from elective selection across degree and exchange programmes to a clash-free timetable, with teaching slots placed directly in faculty Outlook calendars.

The result has been a substantial reduction in manual effort for programme offices and students, and better use of the institute’s resources.

More on the product: Registro Course Bidding and Registro Timetabling.

Two problems, one platform

Course bidding at IIM Bangalore involves hundreds of students across several degree programmes, plus international students on exchange. Managing bid volumes, seat allocation across programmes and fair outcomes by hand was slow and prone to error.

Timetabling added a second layer. Each schedule had to honour faculty preferences, respond to course demand, avoid clashes for students and fit faculty Outlook calendars, all at once. IIM Bangalore needed both problems solved together, not one at a time.

Where coordination broke down

  • High-volume bidding for degree and exchange students with no single system
  • Complex seat-sharing scenarios across programmes and student cohorts
  • Timetables that had to balance demand, faculty preferences and clashes at the same time
  • Faculty preferences collected and applied by hand, slowly and inconsistently
  • No link to faculty Outlook calendars, so conflicts surfaced after publication
  • Significant manual effort for programme offices and students every cycle

How bidding and timetabling work together

EDTEX deployed Registro Course Bidding and Registro Timetabling together. Each solves a distinct problem, and both share one set of data, so bidding outcomes and timetabling inputs stay consistent.

Multi-programme bidding

Bidding across several degree programmes and exchange students, with high bid volumes handled fairly.

Seat allocation across programmes

Seat-sharing and allocation scenarios across cohorts managed in one platform.

Demand-driven timetables

Faculty preferences and student demand fed in, and clash-free timetables generated instantly.

Outlook calendar integration

Teaching slots blocked automatically in faculty Outlook calendars.

Configured for IIM Bangalore

Timetabling set up around the institute’s own scheduling requirements.

Support during every cycle

Prompt support during bidding rounds and timetable scheduling.
