Overview

XLRI implemented Skynet in October 2018 to automate its Day 0 placement process, replacing manual scheduling and student tracking with one integrated platform. When the pandemic forced placements online, EDTEX delivered a virtual placements module, and XLRI ran its Summer Internship Process entirely online without disruption.

Skynet has performed reliably across many placement seasons for students, the Placement Committee, the institute’s administration and recruiting companies.

More on the product: Skynet Placement Automation.

A process built for a different era

XLRI’s placement process means coordinating hundreds of students, many recruiting companies and a placement committee at once, under time pressure. Before Skynet, this relied on spreadsheets, emails and ad hoc scheduling tools, which fragmented the work and limited real-time visibility.

COVID-19 raised the stakes. The institute had to reproduce the full placement experience online without compromising quality, timelines or the experience of students and recruiters.

Where the process was failing

  • Day 0 schedules built by hand, prone to conflicts and coordination errors
  • No real-time tracking of students during placement drives
  • Fragmented communication between students, the placement committee and recruiters
  • No way to run placements virtually during the pandemic
  • A heavy administrative load on the placement office every season

How Skynet changed Day 0

EDTEX deployed Skynet at XLRI to automate the full Day 0 process: structured scheduling, real-time student tracking and coordination of every group in one place. When the pandemic hit, EDTEX built a virtual placements module with its own interfaces for students and companies.

Day 0 automation

Structured scheduling and real-time student tracking across the full Day 0 cycle.

Virtual internship process

XLRI’s Summer Internship Process run entirely online with Skynet’s virtual placements module.

Student and company interfaces

A dedicated portal for each group, so everyone could take part in virtual placements.

Support every season

On-ground support during placement seasons, with issues resolved promptly in every cycle since 2018.
