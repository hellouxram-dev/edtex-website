Overview

NLS Bangalore implemented an elective course bidding system developed by EDTEX, bringing elective selection for its undergraduate and graduate programmes onto one platform. It has made the process more flexible, efficient and transparent for students and administrators.

Students from the integrated BA LLB (Hons.), the LLM and other graduate degrees choose their electives through the same platform, and can shape their studies around their interests and career plans whatever their programme.

More on the product: Registro Course Bidding.

Several programmes, one manual process

NLS Bangalore runs elective selection across several undergraduate and graduate programmes, each with its own credit requirements, seat limits, prerequisites and instructor constraints. Managing this by hand across programme offices took heavy coordination, limited student choice and led to inconsistent allocation between cohorts.

The university needed one system that could handle elective selection across programmes at scale, fairly and transparently, without placing an undue burden on faculty or administrative staff.

Where students and administrators lost time

  • No single platform for elective selection across undergraduate and graduate programmes
  • Manual allocation, prone to conflicts and uneven seat distribution
  • Little visibility for students into course availability, seats and outcomes
  • A heavy workload for faculty and programme staff every registration cycle
  • No way to take electives in another programme, limiting interdisciplinary study
  • No central course information to help students decide

How Registro brought it together

EDTEX implemented Registro Course Bidding at NLS Bangalore, configured to handle every programme on a single system.

All programmes on one platform

BA LLB (Hons.), LLM and all graduate degrees follow the same process.

Electives across programmes

Students bid for electives across programmes, opening up study across law, business, policy and international relations.

Fair allocation

A transparent engine that weighs student preferences against course availability, reducing conflicts.

Real-time updates

Live bid status, seat availability and allocation updates, so students can decide as they go.

Well-filled electives

Enrolment managed so that elective courses fill effectively across all programmes.

The EDTEX team worked closely with NLS Bangalore throughout, tailoring the system to the university’s requirements, supporting it continuously and making the move from the manual process a smooth one.
