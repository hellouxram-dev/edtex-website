Overview

In 2020, O.P. Jindal Global University (OPJGU) partnered with EDTEX to deploy Registro Course Bidding, moving its whole elective bidding process onto time-based selection windows. For the first time, more than 5,000 students across 10+ schools and 45+ programmes could take part in a single registration window at the same time.

Manual effort fell by more than 97% for programme offices and students, and student queries during course registration by 99%. The university can now apply programme-specific credit policies and run multi-term bidding with several elective rounds. Registro has supported more than 30,000 students and 5,000 electives cumulatively since it went live.

More on the product: Registro Course Bidding.

Bidding at a scale manual processes could not reach

Course bidding at OPJGU is among the most complex academic operations in Indian higher education. The university runs multi-term bidding across more than 10 schools, each with its own credit policies, seat-sharing rules and specialisation or concentration requirements.

Before Registro, this needed heavy manual coordination between the IT office and programme teams, which left room for error, inconsistent policy and a high volume of student queries every cycle. The approach could not scale with the university’s growing intake.

What the manual process could not handle

  • No single system for multi-school, multi-term elective bidding
  • Manual coordination between the IT office and programme offices, causing delays and errors
  • Programme-specific credit policies impossible to enforce at scale
  • A high volume of student queries every registration cycle
  • No visibility for students into allocation or seat availability
  • Scaling limits as intake grew across schools and programmes

How Registro took over

EDTEX worked with the IT office and programme offices to design the process, agree best practices and configure Registro for the university’s full complexity: programme credit policies, multi-term rounds, seat sharing across schools and concentrations, in one platform.

Time-based registration windows

More than 5,000 students handled at once in a single real-time bidding window.

Programme credit policies

Each programme’s credit rules and concentration requirements enforced automatically.

Multi-term bidding

Several elective bidding rounds across terms, all on one platform.

Seat sharing and specialisations

Seat sharing across schools, with concentrations and specialisations configured in the platform.

Visibility for students

Students can see how allocation works, which removes uncertainty and the queries it used to generate.

Support at every stage

User guides, live workshops, live chat and help preparing data for every bidding cycle.
