01Introduction

Winspire Educational Solutions Private Limited ("EDTEX", "we", "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on edtex.in and edtex.ai (the "Website"). This Cookie Policy explains what these technologies are, why we use them, and the choices you have. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

02What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, to make them more efficient, and to provide reporting information. "Similar technologies" include embedded scripts, pixels, and local storage that collect information about how you interact with the Website.

03The categories of cookies we use

We group cookies into the categories below. You can accept or reject each non-essential category at any time through our consent banner.

Essential (Required) — Necessary for basic website functionality, such as page navigation, security, and remembering your privacy choices. These are always active and cannot be switched off, as the Website cannot function properly without them.

Analytics — Help us understand how visitors use the Website — for example, which pages are visited and how users move through the site — so we can measure and improve performance. These are only set with your consent.

Marketing — Used to deliver advertising that is more relevant to you and to measure the effectiveness of our campaigns. These are only set with your consent.

Personalization — Remember choices you make (such as settings and preferences) to tailor your experience. These are only set with your consent.

04How we collect this information

We collect cookie and usage information through cookies, embedded scripts (temporary code active only during your session), and communication logs, as described in our Privacy Policy.

05Managing your preferences

When you first visit the Website, our consent banner lets you accept all, reject all, or choose by category. Non-essential cookies are disabled by default until you consent.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time by reopening the privacy settings from the consent banner. You can also control cookies through your browser settings — most browsers let you block or delete cookies — though disabling some cookies may affect how the Website functions.

If you are in a region governed by "do not sell or share" rules, you may exercise that choice through the consent banner where applicable.

06How long cookies are stored

Cookies remain on your device for varying periods depending on their type. Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser; persistent cookies remain until they expire or you delete them. Your consent record is retained for 436 months, or until you clear your cookies, whichever is sooner.

07Third-party cookies

Some cookies may be set by third-party services we use (for example, analytics or embedded content providers). These providers process data in accordance with their own privacy policies. We use a consent-management platform to record and manage your preferences.

08Legal basis and compliance

We process cookie data in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the Information Technology Act, 2000 and rules made thereunder, and other applicable laws.

09Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
