Placements

What placement challenges do students face without modern IT systems?

Missed updates, manual CVs and clashing interviews: the placement challenges students face without modern IT systems, and how ISB and IIM Trichy fixed them.

By Arun Korupolu, Co-founder & CRO

On this page

  1. What is placement season like for a student without proper systems?
  2. What are the main placement challenges students face?
  3. How does placement automation change the student experience?
  4. What remains the institution's job?
  5. What should students expect from a modern placement process?
  6. Frequently asked questions

The short answer: without modern IT systems, students spend placement season chasing information instead of preparing for it. They miss updates, redo CVs by hand, lose track of where their applications stand and face clashing interviews on placement day. The uncertainty adds stress at exactly the point in the programme when students can least afford it. Institutions that automate the process, such as ISB and IIM Trichy, give students automatic CV generation, live application status and structured offer workflows instead.

This article looks at placement season from the student's side: what goes wrong without proper systems, why it matters, and what changes when the process is digital.

What is placement season like for a student without proper systems?

Picture a final-year MBA student at the start of placements. Announcements arrive by email, on a noticeboard and across several messaging groups. A company moves its interview date, and the update reaches some students and not others. The CV submitted last week was in the wrong format, so it has to be redone by hand. The student has applied to several companies but cannot tell which applications are still live. On placement day, two shortlists are scheduled for the same slot.

None of this is about the student's ability. It is about the process around them. Every hour spent chasing information is an hour not spent preparing for an interview.

What are the main placement challenges students face?

Missed updates and miscommunication

Without a single channel, students struggle to stay updated with placement schedules, deadlines and company announcements. Miscommunication between the placement cell and students about interview dates and requirements is common. Communication between companies and the placement cell can be just as patchy, and students feel the effect when a misunderstanding changes a schedule at short notice.

Lost CVs and repetitive applications

When resumes are handled manually, errors creep in and documents get lost. Each company's application can feel like starting again, with the same details entered repeatedly. Errors in manual application submissions lead to missed opportunities, and it is usually the student, not the process, who pays for them.

No clear view of where applications stand

Inaccurate tracking of applications and interview statuses leaves students guessing. They find it hard to track their own progress through the season. Companies, meanwhile, struggle to sort through large volumes of applications, and the placement cell lacks the reports it needs to analyse and improve the process.

Clashing interviews and exams

Poor coordination produces overlapping interview dates and times. Students managing multiple interviews, and sometimes exams, in the same window have to choose between them, or run between rooms and arrive late to both.

Scattered resources and thin preparation

Company profiles, study materials and previous years' interview experiences are often inconsistently available, with no central platform for placement information. Students lack clear information on skill requirements and job roles, mock tests and interview practice are limited, and there are few systematic ways to assess readiness or learn from feedback on previous interviews. Opportunities to connect with alumni, industry professionals and peers are thin.

Unequal access

Students with disabilities face additional barriers in accessing placement information and opportunities. Students in remote or under-resourced areas can find placement resources harder to reach.

Lost time and added stress

Excessive time spent on manual processes eats into preparation and skill development. Poor time allocation at each stage causes delays and bottlenecks. The combined effect is stress and anxiety, driven by uncertainty and the lack of a clear view of the process and its outcomes.

How does placement automation change the student experience?

Automation does not remove the pressure of placement season. It removes the avoidable part of it. Here is what that looks like at institutions using Skynet.

Profiles and CVs that are right the first time

At ISB, Skynet handles student master profile creation, data validation and the e-profile book, and generates CVs automatically in ISB's institutional format. Students keep one validated profile instead of reformatting documents for each company. The deployment automates 16+ placement workflows across the Hyderabad and Mohali campuses and has reduced manual work by 80%, with improved transparency and real-time visibility for students and recruiters. Read the ISB case study.

Offer rules applied consistently

When offers are managed manually, students face uncertainty about where they stand after an offer and whether decisions are applied consistently. At ISB and IIM Trichy, dream offer workflows and offer management run within the platform, structured and tracked rather than handled case by case.

Live application status and attendance that counts

At IIM Trichy, Skynet provides centralised application tracking with real-time status updates for students, so they can see where each application stands. Pre-placement talks are scheduled with automated notifications and QR code-based attendance tracking for each session, so nobody depends on a paper sign-in sheet. CVs are generated automatically in the institute's format. Read the IIM Trichy case study.

Clash-free placement days

At IIT Bombay, 900 students were interviewed on Day 0 across 40 companies with zero schedule clashes. For how that works on the day, see why real-time tracking on Day 0 and Day 1 is critical.

Participation that does not depend on being in the room

When placements moved online, IIM Lucknow onboarded 500 students onto virtual hiring platforms before placement day, with round-the-clock EDTEX support during simulations and live placement runs. The portal was praised by both recruiters and students for its simplicity and ease of use. Read the IIM Lucknow case study.

XLRI went further, running its Summer Internship Process 100% virtually on Skynet, with dedicated interfaces for students and companies. Read the XLRI case study.

What remains the institution's job?

Some challenges on the list are not solved by software alone. Mock interviews, feedback on previous interviews, alumni networking and support for students with disabilities depend on the placement committee, faculty and the wider institution. What a proper system does is give students and the committee back the time now lost to manual work, so more of it goes to preparation and support.

What should students expect from a modern placement process?

  • One place for schedules, deadlines and company announcements.
  • A profile entered once, validated, and turned into a CV in the institution's format.
  • Live status for every application and shortlist.
  • Offer rules applied by the system, the same way for everyone.
  • Interview schedules without clashes.
  • The option to take part virtually when needed.

If your institution's process falls short on several of these, the student experience is paying for it.

Frequently asked questions

What are the biggest placement challenges for students without IT systems?

Missed or conflicting communication, manual CV handling, no visibility of application status, clashing interviews, and the stress that comes from not knowing what is happening.

How does automatic CV generation help students?

Students maintain one validated master profile and the system generates the CV in the institution's format, as at ISB and IIM Trichy. That removes formatting errors and repeated work.

Can students track their placement applications in real time?

Yes, with a platform such as Skynet. At IIM Trichy, students get real-time status updates on their applications.

Does placement automation work for virtual placements?

Yes. XLRI ran its Summer Internship Process 100% virtually on Skynet, and IIM Lucknow onboarded 500 students onto virtual hiring platforms before placement day.

Does automation replace the placement committee?

No. It removes repetitive work such as formatting CVs, taking attendance and resolving clashes, so the committee can spend more time on recruiters and on students who need support.
