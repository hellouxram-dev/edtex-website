Overview

IIM Raipur adopted Registro Course Bidding in 2024 to digitise elective bidding for its MBA/PGP programme. From the first cycle, it introduced multi-term bidding, clash-free timetable generation and automatic enforcement of credit policies.

A distinctive part of the implementation is that timetable generation is built into the bidding process itself. Clashes are resolved before students bid, which widens the range of electives the institute can offer. The platform has also improved how rooms and faculty are used, helping the institute plan as intake grows.

More on the product: Registro Course Bidding.

Multi-term complexity, with no system to manage it

At IIM Raipur, MBA/PGP students choose electives across several terms, each with its own credit requirements, add/drop rounds and programme-specific policies. Managing this by hand created heavy coordination work for the PGP office. Timetable clashes went unnoticed until after allocation, and every registration cycle brought a high volume of student queries.

The institute needed one platform that could enforce credit policies automatically, generate clash-free timetables before bidding opened, run multi-term and multi-round bidding, and show students exactly how allocation works, without adding to the programme office's workload.

Where every cycle broke down

  • No single platform for multi-term, multi-programme elective bidding
  • Demand surveys and course clash matrices prepared by hand before each cycle
  • Timetable clashes found only after registration had closed
  • Fewer electives on offer because scheduling constraints were unresolved
  • Programme-specific credit policies checked manually
  • Add/drop rounds run manually: slow and prone to error
  • A high volume of student queries every registration cycle

How Registro solved it

EDTEX configured Registro Course Bidding with IIM Raipur's MBA/PGP programme office, agreeing best practices together and covering the institute's full multi-term process.

Demand survey and clash matrix

High-demand electives identified and the clash matrix generated automatically before each bidding cycle.

Clash-free timetables

Timetables generated before bidding opens, widening elective choice and removing post-allocation conflicts.

Multi-term bidding and add/drop

Multiple rounds per term and structured add/drop, all within the platform.

Credit policies enforced

MBA/PGP credit requirements applied automatically in every round and term, with no manual checking.

Seat sharing and concentrations

Seat sharing across programmes and elective concentrations or specialisations configured in the platform.

Support at every stage

User guides, live workshops, policy consultation, live chat during bidding and help preparing data.
