Why EDTEX

When electives, timetables and placements outgrow spreadsheets and email

Deans, programme offices and placement chairs bring us the same problems: scarce seats to allocate fairly, timetables that clash across programmes, and placement days run on shared sheets. These processes decide whether students get the programme they were promised.

EDTEX is a Hyderabad-based company, founded in 2016, that builds academic and placement operations software for business schools, law schools and universities. EDTEX Registro runs course bidding, timetabling, student exchange and the academic record; EDTEX Skynet runs placements from the first recruiter contact to the final offer. 40+ institutions and 100,000+ students use them every year, including one in ten of the world’s top 100 business schools.

How long does it take to deploy EDTEX?

Registro Course Bidding typically takes one to four weeks. Registro Timetable Automation typically takes 4 to 15 weeks, depending on your academic policy and constraints.

Which systems does EDTEX integrate with?

ERPs such as Oracle and SAP, student information systems such as Ellucian and Anthology Student, and Salesforce Education Cloud. Single sign-on works through Google Workspace and Microsoft Entra ID.

How does EDTEX protect institutional and student data?

With two-factor authentication, single sign-on, encryption and access controls. Registro and Skynet are designed to support compliance with GDPR and India’s DPDP Act.

Can we start with one module?

Yes. Registro and Skynet can be used separately or together. EDTEX recommends where to begin after understanding how your office runs today.
