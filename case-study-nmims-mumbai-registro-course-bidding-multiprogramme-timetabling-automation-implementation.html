Overview

NMIMS Mumbai partnered with EDTEX in 2019 to digitise course bidding for its MBA programme on Registro, replacing a manual, fragmented process with real-time elective selection and policies applied automatically. Manual effort fell by more than 95% and student queries by 99%.

Registro has since run five multi-term bidding cycles at NMIMS Mumbai, for more than 2,500 students and 500 electives cumulatively. It also gives the institute better use of its resources and helps it plan for a growing intake.

More on the product: Registro Course Bidding and Registro Timetabling.

Multi-trimester complexity, with no automation

Course bidding at NMIMS Mumbai involves hundreds of MBA students across several trimesters, each with its own credit policies, concentration requirements and timetable constraints. Workshop registration and timetable scheduling also had to be managed alongside bidding, which made coordination harder still.

Before Registro, the MBA programme office did most of this by hand, from demand surveys and clash matrices to waitlists and policy checks, with no single system for the full cycle of elective selection.

What the manual process could not handle

  • No single platform for multi-term, multi-round elective bidding across MBA programmes
  • Demand surveys and course clash matrices prepared by hand, causing delays
  • Timetable clashes not detected at the time of bidding
  • No automatic movement for students on waitlists
  • MBA concentration rules for electives not enforceable
  • A high volume of student queries every registration cycle
  • Add/drop rounds coordinated by hand between MBA offices and students

How Registro handled it

EDTEX configured Registro with NMIMS Mumbai's MBA programme offices, agreeing best practices together and covering the full multi-term, concentration-based elective process.

Demand survey and clash matrix

High-demand electives identified and the course clash matrix generated automatically before each bidding cycle.

Clash-free timetables

Timetables generated at the point of bidding, with conflicts resolved before allocation.

Multi-trimester bidding and add/drop

Several bidding rounds per trimester, plus add/drop rounds, all within the platform.

Automatic waitlist movement

Students on waitlists moved into available seats with no manual step.

Concentration-based selection

Concentration and specialisation rules for MBA electives built into Registro.

Credit policies enforced

MBA credit requirements applied automatically in every round and trimester.

EDTEX supported students and MBA programme office staff throughout: user guides in video and document form, live workshops, policy consultation, live chat during bidding windows and help preparing data.
