Registro · Academic ERP

One system for registration, timetables, grades and the student record

Registro brings registration, course bidding, timetabling, attendance, grading, fees and student exchange into one governed system for the programme office, configured to your policies, programme structures and approval chains.

EDTEX Registro Academic ERP is a student information system that keeps one student record across registration, course bidding, timetabling, attendance, grading, fees and student exchange, configured to each institution’s academic policies and approval chains. It is in use at IIMs, ISB, SDA Bocconi and TAPMI.

What it does

Most programme offices run on spreadsheets, disconnected tools and email. Registro replaces them with one student record and modules that share the same data.

One student record

A single, accurate record of every student, course, grade and requirement.

  • One source of truth across all programmes
  • Grades, progress, prerequisites and degree requirements tracked
  • Changes reflected across every connected module in real time

Modules that work together

Registration, bidding, timetabling and exchange as one connected system.

  • Course registration and elective bidding with credit rules enforced
  • Timetabling that respects faculty, room and section constraints
  • Inbound and outbound exchange, with no re-entry of data

Your policies, configured once

Rules are encoded once and enforced everywhere.

  • Credit-allocation rules, prerequisites and programme-specific policies
  • Caps, quotas and specialisation rules without manual checks
  • Updated when academic policy or accreditation needs change

Many programmes, one platform

MBA, executive, doctoral and undergraduate programmes, each with its own structure, credit policy and workflow, within the scope agreed with your programme offices.

Queries in one traceable place

A built-in query management system lets students raise and track questions with programme office advisors, replacing scattered emails with a logged record.

Reports for leadership and accreditation

Student performance, enrolment trends and resource utilisation, plus custom reports defined by your programme office. Students and staff sign in with Google or Microsoft single sign-on.

How Registro is implemented

Scope is agreed with your programme offices up front, so the system reflects how your institution already runs.

  1. Needs assessmentA review of your academic policies and credit-allocation rules with the programme office.
  2. ConfigurationRegistro set up to your workflows, within the agreed product scope.
  3. IntegrationConnection to your existing systems through APIs.
  4. TestingEach workflow checked before anyone relies on it.
  5. Training and go-liveUsers trained before go-live, with live support in critical windows.

Common questions

Answers from our product team. For anything else, ask us during the discovery call.

How is Registro Academic ERP implemented?

In stages: needs assessment, configuration to your academic policies, integration with existing systems, testing and user training before go-live. Scope is agreed with your programme offices up front, so the system reflects how your institution already runs.

Can Registro manage multiple academic programmes?

Yes. Registro Academic ERP manages diverse programmes, such as MBA, executive, doctoral and undergraduate, each with its own requirements, within the functional scope agreed with your programme offices.

Can Registro adapt when academic policies change?

Yes. Registro can be updated to reflect changes in academic policies and procedures, so the system keeps pace with your institution rather than forcing workarounds.

How does Registro help students communicate with the programme office?

Registro includes a query management system that lets students raise and track queries with programme office advisors in one place, replacing scattered emails with a traceable record.

How does Registro Academic ERP keep student data secure?

With two-factor authentication, single sign-on through Google Workspace and Microsoft Entra ID, encryption and access controls. It is designed to support compliance with GDPR and India’s DPDP Act.

How do users get help?

Users can turn to FAQs, user guides and a help desk for technical support. During registration and other critical windows, EDTEX provides live support so issues are resolved while students are still in the process.
