Case study: Registro Course Bidding

Overview

IIM Indore adopted Registro Course Bidding in 2017, one of the earliest premier management institutes in India to digitise elective bidding. With more than 3,000 students across its PGP and IPM programmes, it is also one of EDTEX’s largest implementations. Manual effort is down 95%, student queries are down 99%, and satisfaction has stayed above 4.5 out of 5 for eight years.

A defining feature is dual-level credit tracking: Registro enforces elective credits at term level and academic-year level at the same time, across two programmes with different credit structures. The PGP office has a single view of every student and elective in each cycle.

More on the product: Registro Course Bidding.

Scale and complexity from day one

Course bidding at IIM Indore spans two programmes, PGP and IPM, each with its own credit policies, elective requirements and cohort size. Enforcing these by hand for both programmes at once, while tracking credits by term and by year, did not scale as intake grew.

EDTEX worked with the PGP Programme Office to design the process, agree best practices and configure Registro for the institute’s multi-programme needs, a collaboration that has now run for eight years.

Where the process was straining

  • No single platform for bidding across PGP and IPM at the same time
  • Credit policies enforced by hand across two programmes with different structures
  • No way to track and enforce elective credits at both term and academic-year level
  • Multiple bidding rounds across programmes, unmanageable without automation
  • A high volume of student queries every registration cycle
  • No data-driven basis for planning resources against a growing intake

How Registro met the complexity

EDTEX configured Registro Course Bidding for IIM Indore’s multi-programme structure, including the dual-level credit tracking its academic structure requires.

Two programmes, one platform

PGP and IPM managed at the same time, each with its own credit policies, cohort and bidding rounds.

Dual-level credit tracking

Elective credits tracked and enforced at term level and academic-year level at once, across both programmes.

Credit policies enforced

Programme credit requirements applied automatically in every bidding round.

Multi-round bidding

Multiple elective bidding rounds across both programmes in one platform.

Built for scale

The platform has kept pace as intake has grown since 2017.

Support at every stage

User guides, live workshops, policy consultation, live chat during bidding and help preparing data.
