EDTEX Registro Timetable Automation generates clash-free timetables across programmes, rooms and faculty in minutes rather than weeks, meeting every hard constraint and balancing preferences such as faculty slots. IIM Calcutta cut timetable build time by 70% with it, and IIM Lucknow auto-resolved more than 90% of clashes. It is in use at IIM Bangalore, IIM Kozhikode, IIM Lucknow, IIM Calcutta, TAPMI, KJ Somaiya and NMIMS.

What it does

Registro takes in your courses, faculty, rooms, student groups and rules, and produces a schedule your academic team can check and trust, replacing weeks of spreadsheet work.

Schedules that respect every rule

The engine treats some rules as fixed and others as preferences to meet wherever possible.

  • Hard constraints: room capacity, instructor availability, section clashes
  • Soft constraints: preferred slots, balanced faculty loads, minimal student gaps
  • Competing priorities weighed to give the best achievable schedule

~90% elimination of scheduling conflicts across hard constraints, in minutes rather than weeks.

Faculty preferences and teaching load

Collected once and built into every version of the timetable.

  • Preferred teaching slots and availability captured in the system
  • No clashes across all the courses one faculty member teaches
  • Loads balanced to avoid back-to-back marathons

Regeneration when things change

When faculty, rooms or offerings change, the timetable is regenerated rather than rebuilt by hand.

  • Clashes detected and resolved against your rules and priorities
  • Unresolved conflicts shown with the classes, courses and students affected
  • Built-in tools to reschedule clashing courses

Many programmes and campuses

Each programme and campus keeps its own rules, calendar and rooms, while shared faculty and rooms are scheduled together in one run. Electives shared by MBA, undergraduate and doctoral students are placed once, without clashing with any programme’s core courses.

Live schedules for students and faculty

Students and faculty see their personal timetables in a portal that updates in real time when a class is moved, cancelled or rescheduled.

Reports on rooms and load

Reports on each generated timetable, covering room utilisation, faculty load and how conflicts were resolved, for leadership and accreditation.

How a timetable is produced

The programme office stays in control at each step; the system does the combinatorial work.

  1. Set upUpload courses, faculty, rooms, cohorts, time and week slots and holidays. Configure hard and soft constraints once.
  2. GenerateProduce conflict-free timetables for one or many programmes, with competing priorities weighed automatically.
  3. AdjustRegenerate when faculty availability, rooms or course offerings change, without starting again.
  4. PublishRelease final schedules to students and faculty in one workflow, with changes reflected in real time.

Questions about Registro Timetable Automation

Short answers from our team. Anything else, ask us on the discovery call.

See all questions

What data does Registro need to generate a timetable?

Courses and sections, faculty and their preferences, rooms and capacities, student groups, active time slots and teaching weeks, holidays, and your institution’s hard and soft constraints. This data can be imported from your existing ERP or spreadsheets.

How does Registro prevent faculty and room clashes across programmes?

Registro treats faculty, classrooms and time slots as institution-wide resources and schedules every programme in a single optimisation run. A professor or room can be assigned only once per slot, so one programme’s timetable can never create a clash in another’s.

Can Registro build a combined timetable for electives shared across programmes?

Yes. Common electives taken by students from different programmes, such as MBA, undergraduate and doctoral cohorts, are scheduled in a single timetable. A shared elective is placed once without clashing with any participating programme’s core courses.

How does Registro handle hard and soft constraints?

Hard constraints, such as room capacity, faculty availability and no student clashes, are rules every timetable must satisfy. Soft constraints, such as preferred time slots, are preferences to meet wherever possible. The optimiser first guarantees every hard constraint, then satisfies as many soft constraints as it can.

How long does it take to deploy?

Deploying Registro Timetable Automation typically takes between 4 and 15 weeks. EDTEX tailors the system to your academic policy, active courses and constraints; the exact timeline depends on the complexity and scope of customisation needed.

Can Registro integrate with our ERP or student information system?

Yes. Registro integrates with university ERPs and student information systems, including Oracle, SAP, Ellucian and Anthology Student, through APIs and data import/export. Course, faculty and student data flow in and the published timetable flows back. The exact scope is agreed with your IT team before deployment.
