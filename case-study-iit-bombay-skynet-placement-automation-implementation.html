Overview

IIT Bombay deployed Skynet Scheduler to run Day 0 of its placement season, managing 900 students, 40 companies and 200 volunteers in real time with no interview clashes.

Day 0 is one of the most demanding days in campus recruitment: hundreds of students interviewed at the same time by dozens of companies, with shortlists of 40 to 500 candidates per company. Skynet Scheduler brings clash-free schedules, real-time tracking of interview status and volunteer deployment onto one platform.

More on the product: Skynet Scheduler and Skynet Placement Automation.

900 students, 40 companies, one day

Coordinating Day 0 by hand had always been a large task for the placement cell. Up to 40 companies held interviews for shortlists of 40 to 500 students, and about 900 students had to be interviewed in a single day, so the risk of clashes and missed interviews was high.

Alongside the schedule, 200 volunteers had to be deployed and managed at the same time, with no single system to do it.

What the placement cell was up against

  • Schedules built by hand across 40 companies and 900 students, leading to interview clashes
  • No automatic way to account for student preferences and shortlist load
  • 200 volunteers deployed without optimisation, leaving gaps and confusing students
  • No real-time view of interview status or student progress during the day
  • Last-minute changes unmanageable without a way to adjust schedules live

How Skynet Scheduler runs Day 0

Skynet Scheduler handles scheduling, live changes, interview tracking and volunteer deployment for Day 0 on one platform.

Clash-free scheduling

Matching algorithms generate schedules with no overlaps, taking account of student preferences and each company’s shortlist load.

Live schedule changes

Controls at company and panel level allow adjustments throughout Day 0.

Real-time interview tracking

Live status for every student and interview across all companies through the day.

Volunteer coordination

Volunteers deployed across all interview stations, reducing waiting and confusion.
