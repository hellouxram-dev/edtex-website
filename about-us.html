About EDTEX

Software built only for academic and placement offices

We don’t adapt generic enterprise software for education. Since 2016 we have built exclusively for business schools, law schools and universities, around how their offices actually work.

EDTEX is a Hyderabad company, founded in 2016 by Anil Vishwanadhula and Arun Korupolu, that builds academic and placement operations software for business schools, law schools and universities. It runs two platforms: EDTEX Registro, for programme offices (course bidding, timetabling, student exchange and academic ERP), and EDTEX Skynet, for placement offices. EDTEX works with 40+ institutions, including one in ten of the world’s top 100 business schools.

Why does EDTEX do this work?

We build automation software, including AI, that helps academic and placement offices run more efficiently. It gives students clear digital touchpoints through their programme, and helps universities grow their intake and make better use of faculty, rooms and time.

How has EDTEX grown since 2016?

From one elective bidding system at IIM Ahmedabad to academic and placement software at institutions in India and abroad.

  1. 2016

    The beginning

    We saw that higher education institutions needed software for academic operations, and deployed the Registro course bidding system at IIM Ahmedabad, improving how students choose their electives.

  2. 2017January

    The second customer

    “The second customer proves the validity of your business idea.” We implemented Registro elective course bidding at IIM Calcutta. During that visit, the IIM Calcutta placement representatives described their need for placement automation, and we started building it.

  3. 2018January

    New solutions in the pipeline

    • IIM Bangalore: multi-programme elective course bidding and timetable scheduling, and automation of the academic exchange process.
    • XLRI Jamshedpur: digitised and automated Day 0 and Day 1 placements.
    • IIM Ahmedabad: a solution to digitise and automate the PGP admissions process.
  4. 2020January

    The pandemic year

    • Supported the first successful virtual placements at IIT Bombay and IIM Kozhikode.
    • Skynet was used by 200+ multinational recruiters, supporting about 50,000 virtual interviews across the placement cycles of 10+ premier Indian institutions.
    • 20,000+ students in 50+ graduate programmes chose their electives on Registro course bidding from home.
    • Designed and ran the IC Digital Operations Internship with ISB Hyderabad.
    • EDTEX grew by 300% that year.
  5. 2026

    SDA Bocconi, Milan

    SDA Bocconi School of Management, Milan, onboards Registro Course Bidding.

  6. Today

    Academic ERP, AI timetabling and institutions worldwide

    • Registro Academic ERP is live: an integrated ERP built around the programme office, deployed across independent business schools.
    • Academic planning software is being implemented at business schools around the world.
    • AI-driven timetabling, using our own scheduling algorithms.
    • Building AI answer engines that give decision-makers across the institution timely answers.

Questions about EDTEX

Short answers about the company. Product questions are answered on the FAQ page.

When and where was EDTEX founded?

EDTEX was founded in 2016 in Hyderabad, India, by Anil Vishwanadhula and Arun Korupolu. It began with the Registro course bidding system deployed at IIM Ahmedabad.

What are EDTEX’s two platforms?

EDTEX Registro serves programme offices, with course bidding, timetabling, student exchange and an academic ERP. EDTEX Skynet serves placement offices, from placement operations to Day 0 interview scheduling.

How many institutions use EDTEX?

EDTEX works with 40+ higher education institutions, including one in ten of the world’s top 100 business schools. Clients include IIM Ahmedabad, IIM Bangalore, IIM Calcutta, the Indian School of Business, XLRI Jamshedpur and IIT Bombay.
