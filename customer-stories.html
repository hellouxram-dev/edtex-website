What deans, placement chairs and students say about EDTEX

Deans, placement chairs and students at the institutions we work with, including one in ten of the world’s top 100 business schools. Every quote is reproduced as written.

EDTEX customer stories bring together 18 verbatim quotes from deans, programme chairs, placement chairs and students at 11 institutions, including IIM Ahmedabad, IIM Bangalore, the Indian School of Business and XLRI Jamshedpur. They cover EDTEX Registro for course bidding and timetabling, EDTEX Skynet for placements, and IIM Ahmedabad’s PGP admissions software. Where a written case study exists for the same institution and product, the quote links to it.
