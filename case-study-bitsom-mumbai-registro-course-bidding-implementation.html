Overview

BITSoM Mumbai adopted Registro Course Bidding in 2022 for its very first MBA cohort, which made EDTEX a founding partner in the school’s academic operations. EDTEX worked with the MBA Programme Office to design the process, agree best practices and onboard the first students.

Since then, seven bidding cycles have been run for more than 400 students. Seat sharing, concentrations or specialisations and programme-specific credit policies are handled in the platform as standard, and resource planning has improved as intake has grown.

More on the product: Registro Course Bidding.

Building a bidding process from scratch

BITSoM is a new management school. When it launched its MBA programme there was no elective bidding process to build on. It needed more than software: a partner to help design the process, set policies, and introduce students and staff to a system none of them had used before.

EDTEX took on that role, working with the MBA Programme Office from the start so that the first cohort had an orderly, professional bidding experience.

Starting without a foundation

  • No existing elective bidding process and no historical baseline
  • A first MBA cohort unfamiliar with course bidding, needing full onboarding
  • No programme-specific credit policies yet; they had to be designed and implemented at the same time
  • Programme Office staff new to running elective bidding
  • Seat sharing and concentration or specialisation rules to be set up from first principles

How Registro set the standard

EDTEX and the MBA Programme Office designed the bidding process together before the first cycle, then configured Registro to run it.

Process design and best practices

The full elective bidding process designed with the MBA Programme Office, with policies in place before the first cycle.

First-cohort onboarding

Hands-on onboarding for students and Programme Office staff.

Credit policies enforced

MBA credit requirements configured and applied automatically from the first bidding cycle.

Seat sharing and concentrations

Seat sharing and elective concentrations or specialisations configured from launch.

Support at every stage

User guides, live workshops, policy consultation, live chat during bidding and help preparing data.

Room to grow

The same platform serves larger cohorts without redesigning the process as intake increases.
