Case study: Registro Course Bidding

How Goa Institute of Management opened elective bidding across concentrations

GIM students now browse and bid for electives across several concentrations on one platform, with a transparent allocation process and live updates on bids and seats.

Location

Solution

Elective selection across several concentrations, with no single system, manual allocation and little visibility for students.

What EDTEX did

Built and configured an elective bidding system with GIM for multi-concentration selection, fair allocation and live updates.

Students choose electives across specialisations in one place, and the administrative load on faculty and staff has fallen.

Overview

Goa Institute of Management implemented an elective course bidding system developed by EDTEX. Students browse and bid for electives across several concentrations on a single platform, which supports interdisciplinary learning, fair allocation and real-time transparency during selection.

The system was built in close collaboration with the EDTEX team and tailored to GIM’s requirements for managing several concentrations, giving students a more flexible and personal way to choose their electives.

More on the product: Registro Course Bidding.

Concentrations without one system

Elective selection at GIM spans several concentrations, each with its own courses, prerequisites and student demand. Without one platform, running selection across them was complex for administrators and restrictive for students, who had no structured way to explore and bid for electives outside their own area.

GIM needed a system that could manage several concentrations at once, show students the status of their bids in real time, and allocate seats fairly across every concentration.

Where students lost flexibility

  • No single platform for elective selection across concentrations
  • Students unable to bid for electives outside their primary concentration
  • Manual allocation without a transparent, fair mechanism, which eroded trust in the process
  • No real-time view of bid status or seat availability during the selection window
  • A heavy administrative load on faculty and staff every cycle
  • Little course information available to students at the point of selection

How Registro opened it up

EDTEX developed the system with GIM and provided continuous support throughout implementation, addressing issues promptly so that the move to the new system went smoothly.

Several concentrations, one platform

Students bid for electives across all concentrations in one place, so they can build interdisciplinary paths.

Fair allocation

A transparent allocation engine gives equitable access across concentrations.

Real-time updates

Live bid status, course availability and seat allocation throughout the selection window.

Course information

Prerequisites, credits and course details available in the platform when students choose.
