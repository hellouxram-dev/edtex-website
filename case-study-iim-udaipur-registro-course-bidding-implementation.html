Overview

IIM Udaipur, ranked among the FT Top 100 Master of Management schools, digitised course bidding for its MBA and MBA Analytics programmes with Registro in 2020 and 2021. Manual effort fell by more than 95% and student queries by 99%. The platform has since supported five multi-term bidding cycles for more than 1,200 students and 700 electives.

The institute set four objectives: simpler elective selection for MBA students, fair and transparent allocation, offerings better aligned with student preferences, and less administrative work. All four were met after Registro went live. Resource use has also improved, helping the institute plan for a growing intake.

More on the product: Registro Course Bidding.

Fair allocation, without a system to deliver it

Course bidding at IIM Udaipur involves hundreds of students from more than one MBA programme, each with its own credit policies, timetable constraints and concentration or specialisation requirements. Managing this by hand across programme offices took heavy coordination and left the institute without a fair, scalable way to allocate courses.

After a successful pilot, Registro was rolled out to the full MBA programme, with training sessions for both students and faculty so that everyone could use it well from the start.

What broke every registration cycle

  • No single platform for multi-programme, multi-round elective bidding
  • Demand surveys and course clash matrices prepared by hand, causing delays
  • Timetable clashes not detected or resolved when students chose courses
  • MBA credit policies not enforced automatically
  • A high volume of student queries every registration cycle
  • Unequal access to electives, with no fair allocation mechanism
  • A heavy administrative load on programme office staff in every round

How Registro made bidding work

EDTEX configured Registro Course Bidding with IIM Udaipur's MBA programme offices, agreeing best practices for both the MBA and MBA Analytics programmes.

Demand survey and clash matrix

High-demand electives identified and the course clash matrix generated automatically before each bidding cycle.

Timetable clashes checked at bidding

Clashes detected at the point of bidding, before allocation is finalised.

Multi-round bidding

Several bidding rounds across terms, managed on one platform.

Fair allocation

A transparent algorithm that weighs bid points against course capacity, giving every student fair access.

Real-time bid status

Live updates on bids, course availability and seats, so students can adjust their choices.

Reports on demand

Reports on bidding patterns and course demand to help plan future course offerings.

EDTEX supported students and programme office staff throughout: user guides in video and document form, live workshops, policy consultation, live chat during bidding windows and help preparing data.
