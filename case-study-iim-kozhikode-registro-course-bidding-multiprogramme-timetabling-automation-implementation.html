Overview

IIM Kozhikode implemented an elective course bidding system synchronised with automated timetable scheduling, both developed by EDTEX. Bidding outcomes feed directly into conflict-free timetable generation, so the path from course selection to published schedule is a single process.

The rollout followed a structured sequence: a needs assessment, customisation, a pilot, and full-scale deployment with training for students and faculty.

More on the product: Registro Course Bidding and Registro Timetabling.

Two systems running separately

Before EDTEX, elective bidding and timetable scheduling at IIM Kozhikode were disconnected. Bids were managed with one set of tools and timetables were built separately, by hand, without knowing the bidding outcomes or course demand. The result was timetable conflicts, under-used classrooms and students whose chosen courses clashed.

The institute needed the two processes to work as one, with bidding data informing the timetable automatically and scheduling constraints feeding back into allocation.

Where the gaps appeared

  • No link between bidding outcomes and timetable generation
  • Timetable conflicts found after bidding closed, too late to resolve efficiently
  • Faculty and classroom allocation managed by hand, without regard to demand
  • No real-time updates for students on bid status or course availability
  • Schedule changes for demand or availability meant manual rework
  • A heavy administrative load from running both processes separately

How bidding and timetabling were joined up

EDTEX deployed Registro Course Bidding and Registro Timetabling together, customised after a needs assessment that covered the requirements of students, faculty and administrative staff.

Fair elective allocation

A transparent bidding algorithm for equitable access, with real-time status updates.

Bidding and course information

Students browse, bid and review prerequisites, instructors and schedules in one place.

Conflict-free timetables

Bidding outcomes feed automated timetable generation, with no manual step in between.

Faculty and room allocation

Faculty and classrooms allocated on course demand and availability, reducing idle time.

Dynamic adjustments

Schedules updated when demand or faculty availability changes, without restarting.

Pilot before full launch

A pilot phase validated the system, and training sessions supported adoption.
