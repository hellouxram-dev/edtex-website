EDTEX consulting starts with a review of how your programme or placement office runs a process today, usually through a 30 to 45 minute discovery call. What do institutions ask us to help with?

Four outcomes that every engagement is aimed at, whether it ends in a standard module or a custom build.

How does a consulting engagement run?

Every EDTEX implementation follows the same five stages. Consulting starts at the first: understanding your process before proposing anything.

  1. Process assessmentWe study how your office runs the process today, with the people who run it.
  2. ConfigurationYour academic or placement policies are encoded in the system.
  3. IntegrationConnection to your ERP, student information system and single sign-on.
  4. TestingEvery workflow checked with your team before it goes live.
  5. Training and supportWorkshops and guides for staff and students, with live support during critical windows.

During registration and bidding

  • Live support while students are in the process
  • Help preparing data before windows open

On placement days

  • The team present through Day 0 and Day 1
  • Issues handled in real time, not in a ticket queue

After go-live

  • A dedicated support team and help desk
  • Configuration updated as policies change

How does EDTEX implement its software at an institution?

In five stages: a process assessment with your programme office, configuration to your academic policies, integration with your existing systems, testing, and user training before go-live. Registro and Skynet are each configured to how your institution already runs academics and placements, rather than forcing a generic workflow.

Is EDTEX software built specifically for higher education?

Yes. EDTEX builds its software specifically for higher-education academic operations, rather than adapting a generic enterprise ERP or CRM. Workflows such as bid-point course allocation, multi-programme timetabling and Day 0 placement slotting are built into the product, so institutions configure their policies instead of commissioning custom development.

What training does EDTEX provide for staff and students?

Onboarding workshops, user guides and video walkthroughs for administrators and students, plus live support during working hours. During high-stakes windows, such as course registration, bidding rounds, placement days and exchange allocation, the EDTEX team supports users in real time.

What support does EDTEX offer when users run into problems?

A dedicated support team, user guides and a help desk for every product. During critical windows the team provides live support, because problems on those days affect students directly and cannot wait in a ticket queue.

Which systems does EDTEX integrate with?

ERPs such as Oracle and SAP, student information systems such as Ellucian and Anthology Student, and Salesforce Education Cloud for CRM. Single sign-on works through Google Workspace and Microsoft Entra ID. Data moves through standard APIs and scheduled import/export.
