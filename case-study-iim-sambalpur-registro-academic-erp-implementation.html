Overview

IIM Sambalpur partnered with EDTEX in 2023 to digitise its academic operations on the Registro Student Information System: one platform covering course bidding, timetabling, academic grading and course feedback. This case study looks at course bidding.

From the first cycle, manual effort fell by 95% and student queries by 99%. The platform has since supported multi-term bidding for more than 600 students and 200 electives cumulatively, and has improved how the institute uses its resources as intake grows.

More on the product: Registro Academic ERP and Registro Course Bidding.

Manual processes holding back growth

Course bidding at IIM Sambalpur involves hundreds of MBA students across several terms, each with its own credit requirements, specialisation pathways and scheduling constraints. Before Registro, the PGP office ran it by hand: preparing demand surveys and clash matrices, checking credit policies and answering student queries with no automation.

As intake grew, this became harder to sustain. The institute needed a platform that could enforce programme-specific policies, support multi-term bidding and show students how allocation works, without adding to the programme office’s workload.

Where the friction was building

  • No single platform for multi-term, multi-round MBA elective bidding
  • Demand surveys and course clash matrices prepared by hand before each cycle
  • MBA credit policies not enforced automatically
  • No way to handle seat sharing or concentration-based elective selection
  • A high volume of student queries every registration cycle
  • Little visibility for students into course availability and allocation outcomes
  • Scaling limits as intake continued to grow

How Registro handled course bidding

EDTEX configured Registro course bidding with IIM Sambalpur's MBA/PGP programme offices, agreeing best practices together and covering the institute's multi-term cycles.

Demand survey and clash matrix

High-demand electives identified and the course clash matrix generated automatically before each bidding cycle.

Credit policies enforced

MBA credit requirements applied automatically in every round and term, with no manual checking.

Multi-term bidding

Multi-term cycles with several rounds each, all within one platform.

Seat sharing and concentrations

Seat sharing and MBA elective concentrations or specialisations configured in the platform.

Visibility for students

Students can see how allocation works, which removes uncertainty and the queries it used to generate.

Support at every stage

User guides, live workshops, policy consultation, live chat during bidding and help preparing data.
