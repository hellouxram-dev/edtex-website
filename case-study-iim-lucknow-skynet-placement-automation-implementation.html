Case study: Skynet Placement Automation

IIM Lucknow placements: how Skynet moved the process to virtual hiring

This case study covers placements at IIM Lucknow, which runs its placement process on EDTEX Skynet. When placements went virtual, EDTEX adapted the platform for e-hiring and onboarded 500 students before placement day, with support around the clock.

Overview

IIM Lucknow deployed Skynet to automate and streamline its placement process. When virtual placements became the norm, EDTEX adapted the platform for e-hiring. 500 students were onboarded onto virtual hiring platforms before placement day, and round-the-clock support from EDTEX allowed several batch runs to be completed across the season.

The placement committee singled out Skynet’s communication across recruiters, students and the placement team, and the way it anticipated data requirements and presented them in an easy-to-use format.

More on the product: Skynet Placement Automation.

Also at IIM Lucknow: how Registro Timetabling halved timetable finalisation time.

The shift that had to work

When placements went virtual, IIM Lucknow needed more than a scheduling tool. It needed a platform that could recreate the placement process online, work with every video-conferencing platform, and support a committee managing hundreds of students and companies at once, under time pressure.

The stakes were high: any failure in coordination, data or availability would affect student outcomes and recruiter confidence. The institute needed a partner with the technology and the operational commitment to make the move work.

Where coordination broke down

  • No platform able to support fully virtual placements
  • Communication between students, recruiters and the committee split across tools
  • No structured way to onboard 500 students onto virtual hiring platforms before placement day
  • Complex data requirements, with no easy format for the committee
  • Batch runs needed around the clock, impossible without dedicated support
  • E-hiring unfamiliar to many recruiters and students

How Skynet supported it

EDTEX deployed Skynet with a full set of virtual placement capabilities, backed by operational support for every stakeholder group.

Video-conferencing integration

Student and company plugins that work with all video-conferencing platforms.

Onboarding at scale

500 students onboarded onto virtual hiring platforms before placement day.

Round-the-clock support

EDTEX supported the Placement Committee 24/7 during simulations and live runs, including odd hours.

One communication flow

Students, recruiters and the placement team coordinated in one place.

A simple portal

The IIM Lucknow placement portal was well received by recruiters and students from the first day.

Data ready to use

Data requirements anticipated and presented in easy-to-use formats for the placement team.
