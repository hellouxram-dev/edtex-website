Overview

IIM Rohtak adopted Registro Course Bidding in 2018, making it one of the earliest IIMs to digitise elective bidding. Since then the platform has run 15 complete bidding cycles across more than 1,000 students and 600 electives, and each cycle can now be run in under 60 minutes.

Registro has also improved how the institute uses its resources, helping it plan as intake grows. It handles a high volume of bids each season and keeps the process fair across the PGP student body.

More on the product: Registro Course Bidding.

A manual process that could not keep pace

Course bidding at IIM Rohtak involves hundreds of PGP students choosing electives under time pressure, with credit policies, seat limits and programme requirements all applied at once. Before Registro, the programme office ran this by hand. It was slow and prone to error, created bottlenecks, generated a high volume of student queries and offered no transparent way to allocate seats.

As intake grew, those limits became impossible to ignore. The institute needed a system that could run complete bidding cycles quickly and fairly, without overwhelming the programme office.

What slowed every cycle down

  • No single platform for real-time elective bidding, so each cycle took days
  • Courses allocated by hand, with no fair or transparent mechanism
  • A high volume of student queries every registration cycle
  • PGP credit policies not enforced automatically
  • Little visibility for students into course availability and allocation outcomes
  • A heavy administrative load on the programme office in every bidding round

How Registro made 60-minute cycles possible

EDTEX configured Registro Course Bidding with IIM Rohtak's PGP programme office around the institute's own elective bidding rules. Real-time processing is what allows a complete cycle to run within 60 minutes.

Real-time bidding

A complete elective bidding cycle runs end to end in under 60 minutes, with hundreds of bids processed at the same time.

Automated, fair allocation

A transparent allocation mechanism gives every PGP student equal access to electives, with no manual step at allocation.

Credit policies enforced

PGP credit requirements applied automatically in every bidding round, with no manual checking.

Visibility for students

Students can see how allocation works, which removes uncertainty and the queries it used to generate.

Scales with intake

The same 60-minute cycle time is maintained as student numbers grow.

Support at every stage

User guides, live workshops, policy consultation, live chat during bidding and help preparing data.
