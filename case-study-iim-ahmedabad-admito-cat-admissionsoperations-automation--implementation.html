Overview

EDTEX developed and deployed purpose-built PGP admissions software for IIM Ahmedabad. It automates the admissions cycle from candidate shortlisting and personal interview scheduling through to the final selection list, and includes support for RTI queries so the process stays transparent and auditable.

The admissions office expressed strong satisfaction with the software and with the EDTEX team, citing efficiency, transparency and on-time delivery, and has recommended EDTEX to other institutions.

See all EDTEX products.

One of the most scrutinised selection processes in India

PGP admissions at IIM Ahmedabad run through several stages: shortlisting candidates, scheduling personal interviews across a large applicant pool, and producing the final list. Each needs precision, fairness and a full audit trail.

Running these stages by hand created risk at every step and made it harder for the admissions office to respond quickly to queries, including RTI requests.

Where the process needed support

  • Manual shortlisting: slow and open to inconsistency
  • Personal interview scheduling with no central, automated system
  • The final selection list needed a great deal of manual coordination
  • Limited auditability, which made RTI responses difficult
  • Heavy administrative effort for the admissions office throughout the cycle

How EDTEX automated it

EDTEX integrated and automated every critical stage of the admissions process in one user-friendly platform, reducing time and effort for the admissions office while keeping each stage transparent and auditable.

Automated shortlisting

Shortlisting integrated and automated, with selection criteria applied consistently.

Interview scheduling

Personal interviews scheduled across a large applicant pool without manual intervention.

Final selection list

The final list of selected candidates generated automatically, accurately and on schedule.

RTI query support

Support for RTI queries built into the platform, with an audit trail across the cycle.

How the team worked

The EDTEX team grasped complex requirements across several areas and prioritised them carefully. Cross-validation and open communication kept the work accurate, and every deliverable was completed on time, including periods when the team worked overtime to meet the admissions schedule.
