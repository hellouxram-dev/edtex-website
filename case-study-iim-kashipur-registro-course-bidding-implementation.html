Overview

IIM Kashipur was among the first new-generation IIMs to digitise multi-programme elective bidding, bringing MBA and MBA Analytics onto Registro Course Bidding in 2020 and 2021. Manual effort fell by 95% and student queries by 99%. Four multi-term cycles have since been run for more than 1,200 students and 700 electives, including seat sharing for global electives.

Registro handles the full multi-programme process in one system: programme-specific credit policies, seat sharing, concentrations or specialisations and workshop registration. It has also helped the institute plan resources as intake in both programmes has grown.

More on the product: Registro Course Bidding.

Two programmes, one manual process

Course bidding at IIM Kashipur spans two programmes, MBA and MBA Analytics, each with its own credit requirements, concentration pathways and cohort. Before Registro, running both at once required heavy coordination between programme offices, with no way to handle seat sharing, cross-programme access or workshop registration alongside elective selection.

As intake grew, the manual approach created bottlenecks and policy inconsistencies, and every registration cycle brought a high volume of student queries, with no transparent allocation process to answer them.

What made every cycle hard

  • No single platform for bidding across MBA and MBA Analytics
  • Manual coordination between programme offices, causing delays and inconsistent policies
  • No automatic enforcement of programme-specific credit policies
  • Seat sharing for global electives unmanageable without structured allocation
  • Workshop registration handled separately from elective bidding
  • A high volume of student queries every registration cycle
  • No transparent allocation; students could not track or understand outcomes

How Registro handled it

EDTEX configured Registro Course Bidding with both the MBA and MBA Analytics programme offices, so the institute’s full multi-programme process runs on one platform.

Two programmes, one platform

MBA and MBA Analytics managed together, each with its own credit policies, cohort and concentration pathways.

Seat sharing for global electives

Seats in global electives shared across programmes without manual allocation.

Credit policies enforced

Credit requirements for both programmes applied automatically in every round and term.

Concentrations and specialisations

Concentration and specialisation pathways configured and enforced in the bidding system.

Workshop registration

Workshop registration handled in the same platform as elective bidding.

Support at every stage

User guides, live workshops, policy consultation, live chat during bidding and help preparing data.
