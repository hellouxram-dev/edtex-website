Overview

IIM Trichy implemented Skynet in 2023, a placement automation platform EDTEX customised to fit the institute’s workflows. It automates more than 12 placement processes end to end, from student profile creation and CV generation to pre-placement talks, shortlisting, dream offer workflows and custom analytics, in a dedicated single-tenant cloud environment.

Skynet was configured in close collaboration with the placement office so that every workflow reflects the institute’s own requirements and standards, for students, recruiters and the placement team in every season.

More on the product: Skynet Placement Automation.

A process that needed its own platform

IIM Trichy’s placement process runs through a long sequence of events, from pre-placement talks and profile shortlisting to interview coordination and offer management. Each stage involves several groups of people and has to be coordinated precisely under time pressure.

Without one platform, this work relied on manual processes across disconnected tools. The placement office could not track candidates in real time, manage recruiter relationships efficiently or produce the analytics needed to review and improve outcomes from one season to the next.

Where the gaps were showing

  • No central platform for student profiles, applications and shortlisting
  • CVs prepared by hand: slow and inconsistent from one student to the next
  • Pre-placement talks run without automated notifications or QR-based attendance
  • Offers managed by hand, with no dream offer workflow
  • Few tools for recruiters: job postings, profile access and schedules not coordinated
  • No placement dashboard or custom reports for the placement office
  • No custom access controls: every user had the same permissions

A Skynet instance built around IIM Trichy

EDTEX deployed a customised instance of Skynet at IIM Trichy, designed around the institute’s placement workflows and configured to cover every stage of the recruitment cycle.

Student profiles and CVs

Master profile creation, data validation and automatic CV generation in IIM Trichy’s format.

Pre-placement talks

Talks scheduled with automated notifications and QR code attendance for each session.

Applications and shortlisting

Applications tracked in one place, with real-time status for students and shortlist management for the office.

Dream offer workflows

Offers, including dream offers, managed and tracked within the platform.

Recruiter portal

A recruiter portal for job postings, access to student profiles and recruitment schedules.

Dashboards and reports

Placement dashboards and custom reports for tracking performance and planning strategy.

Role-based access

Functional access set by role, keeping data secure across all user groups.

Dedicated cloud

A single-tenant EDTEX cloud environment for performance and data isolation.

Through implementation and every placement season since, the EDTEX team has supported the placement office directly and resolved issues promptly.
