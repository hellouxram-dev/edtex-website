Overview

IIM Calcutta adopted Registro Course Bidding and Registro Timetabling in 2017. It has since run 21 term-level elective bidding cycles for more than 3,000 students and 500 electives. Manual effort is down 95%, student queries are down 99%, and satisfaction has stayed above 4.5 out of 5 across eight years, making this one of EDTEX’s longest-running implementations.

The platform covers the full PGP elective cycle in one system: demand estimation and the clash matrix, clash-free timetables, faculty preference collection, credit policies and add/drop rounds.

More on the product: Registro Course Bidding and Registro Timetabling.

Also at IIM Calcutta: how Registro Timetabling cut timetable creation time by 70%.

Eight years, 21 cycles

Course bidding at IIM Calcutta involves hundreds of PGP students choosing electives across terms, with credit policies, demand estimation and timetable constraints to balance at the same time. Before Registro, the PGP office managed this by hand, which became harder to sustain as intake and the number of courses grew.

EDTEX worked with the PGP office to design the process, configure the system and set best practices that have held across eight years and 21 cycles.

What the manual process could not handle

  • No elective demand survey or clash matrix, so popular courses clashed without anyone noticing
  • Timetables not linked to bidding outcomes; conflicts found after allocation
  • Faculty preferences collected by hand, slow to incorporate and applied inconsistently
  • Programme credit policies enforced manually, with errors at scale
  • Add/drop rounds run without automation
  • No demand estimation rounds to inform timetable slots
  • A high volume of student queries every registration cycle

How Registro covered it

EDTEX configured Registro Course Bidding and Registro Timetabling with the PGP office to cover the whole elective cycle.

Demand survey and clash matrix

High-demand electives identified and the clash matrix generated automatically before each bidding cycle.

Demand estimation and add/drop

Demand estimation rounds and structured add/drop run in the platform.

Credit policies enforced

PGP credit requirements applied automatically in every round and term.

Clash-free timetables

Timetables generated clash-free from bidding outcomes, widening the choice of electives.

Faculty preferences

Preferred lecture slots collected from faculty and built into timetable generation.

Support at every stage

User guides, live workshops, policy consultation, live chat and help preparing data, every cycle since 2017.
