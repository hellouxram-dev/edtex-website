Case studies

How institutions run their academic operations on EDTEX

22 implementations at IIMs, ISB, XLRI, IIT Bombay, law schools and universities: elective bidding, timetabling, academic ERP, placements and admissions, in each institution’s own numbers.

Which institutions use EDTEX?

EDTEX has published 22 case studies from 20 institutions, including IIM Ahmedabad, IIM Bangalore, IIM Calcutta, the Indian School of Business, XLRI Jamshedpur, IIT Bombay, National Law School of India University and O.P. Jindal Global University. They cover EDTEX Registro (course bidding, timetabling and academic ERP), EDTEX Skynet (placements and Day 0 interview scheduling) and IIM Ahmedabad’s PGP admissions software.

Headline results, as reported in each case study

  • 5,000+ students bidding for electives in one window at O.P. Jindal Global University
  • 70% less time to create the timetable at IIM Calcutta
  • 900 students interviewed on Day 0 with no schedule clashes at IIT Bombay
  • Under 60 minutes for a complete elective bidding cycle at IIM Rohtak

Each case study lists the source and scope of its figures in a “Notes on the figures” section. Prefer to hear it from the people involved? Read customer stories from deans, placement chairs and students.
