Inbound and outbound exchange, from nomination to credit transfer, in one workflow

EDTEX Registro EPAS is student exchange management software that runs inbound and outbound exchange in one workflow: partner agreements, nominations, applications, course mapping, learning agreements and credit transfer. It manages semester exchanges, summer programmes and dual degrees, and is used at IIM Ahmedabad and IIM Bangalore, among others.

What it does

Exchange is often one of the most fragmented processes in an institution: email threads, partner portals and a spreadsheet only one person understands. Registro EPAS gives the office a live view of every case and partners a consistent process.

Both directions in one place

One platform for the full mobility lifecycle, with no parallel trackers.

  • Outbound students guided from nomination to application, acceptance and return
  • Inbound students onboarded with registration tailored to your policies
  • Every document, approval and status kept in one record

Partners and agreements

A single home for your exchange partnerships.

  • Partner university profiles and programme factsheets
  • MOUs, agreement status and seat allocations
  • Partners compared on academic quality, programme fit and student feedback

Course mapping and credit transfer

Courses taken abroad recognised without back-and-forth.

  • Course equivalences and credit-mapping approvals in a governed flow
  • Academic sign-off routed to faculty and coordinators
  • Approved credits and transferred grades recorded, with an audit trail

Students always know where they stand

Students track their application from submission through seat allocation and acceptance, including any steps they still need to complete, and find pre-departure checklists, orientation material and key contacts in one place.

Fewer reminders sent by hand

Automated reminders and a live status view of every student’s case replace manual chasing by the office.

Reports on the exchange portfolio

Participation, partner performance, application trends and programme outcomes, so decisions about which partnerships to grow, renew or retire rest on evidence.

How an outbound exchange runs

The same record follows the student from first search to credit on return.

  1. ExploreBrowse partner universities and factsheets, and compare academic fit, location and credit compatibility.
  2. PrepareCheck course equivalence and credit criteria, and read the pre-departure material.
  3. ApplySubmit nominations and applications through a guided flow, with documents in one place.
  4. TrackFollow submission, seat allocation, acceptance and next steps in real time.
  5. ReturnHave courses taken abroad recognised through a structured credit-transfer process.

Registrars and leadership

Questions about Registro student exchange

Short answers from our team. Anything else, ask us on the discovery call.

See all questions

What is student exchange management software?

Software that runs an institution’s outbound and inbound exchange programmes: partner information, student applications, seat allocation, credit transfer and pre-departure preparation. It replaces spreadsheets and email-based processes that depend on one or two people in the international office.

Which exchange programmes can Registro EPAS manage?

Semester exchanges, summer programmes and dual-degree programmes, each with its own requirements, for both incoming and outgoing students.

How does Registro EPAS handle credit transfer for courses taken abroad?

Registro EPAS records the information needed to manage credit transfer and course equivalencies, so students receive the right recognition for courses completed at a partner institution.

How does Registro EPAS help compare partner universities?

It includes partner university factsheets that let students and staff compare partners on academic quality, programme compatibility and student feedback.

Can students track their exchange application?

Yes. Students track their application from submission through seat allocation and acceptance, including any additional steps they need to complete.

How does Registro EPAS protect student data?

Student personal data is protected with encryption and access controls, and the platform is designed to support compliance with GDPR and other applicable data protection regulations, which matters when records move between institutions in different countries.
