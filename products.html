The EDTEX platform has two products. EDTEX Registro runs academic operations for programme offices and deans: course bidding, timetable automation, student exchange (EPAS) and the academic ERP. EDTEX Skynet runs placements for placement committees and career services, including Day 0 interview scheduling. Both are used by 40+ institutions and 100,000+ students, and can be adopted one module at a time.

Registro

For programme offices, registrars and deans

Academic operations from course demand to transcripts, configured to your academic policy, credit rules and programme structures.

  1. Estimate demandStudents signal interest in planned electives.
  2. Run bidding roundsBids, waitlists and add/drop under your rules.
  3. Build the timetableClash-free across programmes, rooms and faculty.
  4. Keep the recordRegistration, grades, attendance and transcripts.

What is EDTEX?

EDTEX is a Hyderabad-based company, founded in 2016, that builds academic operations software for business schools, law schools and universities. Its products are Registro (academic ERP, timetabling, course bidding and student exchange) and Skynet (placement automation). EDTEX works with 40+ institutions, including 10% of the global Top 100 business schools.

Is EDTEX software built specifically for higher education?

Yes. EDTEX builds its software specifically for higher-education academic operations, rather than adapting a generic enterprise ERP or CRM. Workflows such as bid-point course allocation, multi-programme timetabling and Day 0 placement slotting are built into the product, so institutions configure their policies instead of commissioning custom development.

Can we start with one module?

Yes. Registro and Skynet can be used separately or together. We will recommend where to begin after understanding how your office runs today.

Which systems does EDTEX integrate with?

ERPs such as Oracle and SAP, student information systems such as Ellucian and Anthology Student, and Salesforce Education Cloud for CRM. Single sign-on works through Google Workspace and Microsoft Entra ID. Data moves through standard APIs and scheduled import/export.

How does EDTEX implement its software?

In five stages: a process assessment with your office, configuration to your policies, integration with your existing systems, testing, and user training before go-live.

How does EDTEX keep institutional and student data secure?

With two-factor authentication, single sign-on through Google Workspace and Microsoft Entra ID, encryption and access controls. The platforms are designed to support compliance with GDPR and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act.
